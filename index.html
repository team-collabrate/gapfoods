Our Story

From home baking to a trusted wholesale bakery

GAP Foods began as a home baking venture, built on the simple idea that bakery food should taste indulgent without compromising on ingredients.

Today, we operate a dedicated quality-focused manufacturing facility in Coimbatore, supplying consistent, health-conscious bakery products in bulk to cafés, canteens and event partners across Tamil Nadu, carrying the same promise into every batch: unadulterated love.

  • Consistent recipes, batch after batch
  • Scalable for bulk & custom orders
  • Hygiene-first production process
